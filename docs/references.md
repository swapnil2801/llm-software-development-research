# References

The following sources were used to ground the research paper. Access dates should be added according to the college's required citation style.

1. **Peng, S. et al. (2023).** *The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot.* Controlled experiment with 95 professional developers completing a JavaScript HTTP-server task; reports a 55.8% faster completion time for the treatment group. [arXiv:2302.06590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590)

2. **Hou, X. et al. (2024).** *Large Language Models for Software Engineering: A Systematic Literature Review.* ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology. Review of 395 articles covering LLMs and software-engineering tasks. [DOI: 10.1145/3695988](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3695988)

3. **He, J., Treude, C., and Lo, D. (2024).** *LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems for Software Engineering: Literature Review, Vision and the Road Ahead.* Review of primary studies and future research directions for collaborative LLM agents in software engineering. [arXiv:2404.04834](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.04834)

4. **Krishna, A. et al. (2025).** *Importing Phantoms: Measuring LLM Package Hallucination Vulnerabilities.* Examines invented package references and their software-supply-chain implications. [arXiv:2501.19012](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19012)

5. **Microsoft Research / MIT / Princeton / Wharton authors (2024).** *Dear Diary: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Generative AI Coding Tools in the Workplace.* Workplace randomized study of coding-assistant use and developer outcomes. [arXiv:2410.18334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18334)

6. **Zhang, Q. et al. (2025).** *A Survey on Large Language Models for Software Engineering.* Science China Information Sciences. Survey of LLMs of Code, software-engineering tasks, evaluation, security, and reliability. [Springer DOI: 10.1007/s11432-025-4670-0](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11432-025-4670-0)

7. **Jimenez, C. E. et al. (2024).** *SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-world GitHub Issues?* ICLR 2024. Introduces a benchmark based on real repository issues and shows why multi-file maintenance tasks are substantially harder than isolated code generation. [arXiv:2310.06770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770)

8. **Cui, M. et al. (2025).** *The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers.* Reports a pooled analysis of 4,867 developers across workplace experiments. [MIT working-paper PDF](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/draft_copilot_experiments.pdf)

9. **Peng, S. et al. (2024).** *How Much Does AI Impact Development Speed? An Enterprise-based Randomized Controlled Trial.* Reports an enterprise study with 96 Google software engineers and discusses the difference between controlled and workplace evidence. [arXiv:2410.12944](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12944)

10. **Autio, C. et al. (2024).** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile.* NIST AI 600-1. Provides a governance framework organized around governing, mapping, measuring, and managing generative-AI risks. [NIST DOI: 10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/nist.ai.600-1)

11. **NIST (2024).** *Secure Software Development Practices for Generative AI and Dual-Use Foundation Models: An SSDF Community Profile.* Extends secure-development practices to AI model and AI-system development. [NIST SP 800-218A](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218A)

12. **Stack Overflow (2025).** *2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey — AI.* Industry survey reporting usage, trust, frustration, and concerns around AI tools. It is a developer survey, not controlled causal evidence. [Survey AI section](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai)
